      Know the forest

      ❋
      🦊FoxShortest-path chaser. Avoids bushes, water and rocks.
      🦅EagleFlies over everything, moves diagonally, rests every 3rd day.
      🐅TigerMoves twice while chasing, then rests.
      🐆LeopardClimbs rocks, so rocks are not safe from it.
      🐍SnakeSlow, but finds you in bushes and water.
      🐊CrocodileStays in ponds and bites nearby.
      🦌DeerHarmless, eats grass and blocks your way.
      GrassFood. Grows back after 15 days.
      PondWalk into it to drink. Less water during drought.
      BushHiding place (not for turtle).
      RockSquirrel, leopard and eagle can be here.
      Burnt groundLeft behind after a forest fire.
